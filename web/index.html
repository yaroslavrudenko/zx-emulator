Shift is CAPS SHIFT · Tab or Ctrl is SYMBOL SHIFT · F1 readout · F2 save a .z80 · F3/F4/F5 tape · F6 reset · F7 arrow keys · drop a .tap, .z80 or .sna anywhere on this page to load it.

The Spectrum has no arrow keys, and games disagree about what to read — so F7 cycles what the arrows press, and the current choice is shown at the bottom of the screen. cursor is right for BASIC; 5678 is what most games mean by “cursor keys” and is the one for Manic Miner; QAOP suits Cybernoid and Exolon; Kempston drives the joystick port, which no game reads by accident. Sound starts on your first click or keystroke.
